Our Story

Driving Uganda's Commerce Forward

Keron Motors has been at the forefront of providing reliable and efficient Tuk Tuk cargo tricycles across Kampala, Mbarara, Jinja, and beyond since 2010. We understand the vital role these vehicles play in local commerce, agriculture, and logistics.

Our mission is simple: to keep your business moving. We are committed to offering top-quality vehicle sales, expert servicing, and genuine spare parts, ensuring your fleet operates at peak performance with minimal downtime.
